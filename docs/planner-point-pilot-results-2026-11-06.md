# Student-state point-route pilot: stopped before games

The [prospective protocol](planner-point-pilot-protocol-2026-11-06.md) selected 15 TRAIN and seven label-validation student-reached states. Blind strategic review and public-consistent route checks passed its pre-fit gate: all 22 proposed planner roots belonged to supported accepted sets, eight TRAIN sets excluded cached H, and eight excluded the student action. The route checks certified the selected planner point routes in synthetic public-consistent states. They did **not** replay original hidden states or fully validate every accepted alternative. The [posthoc audit limitation](../runs/swarm-2026-11-04/agent-research/planner-point-blind-pilot-001/fit/POSTHOC-AUDIT-LIMITATION.md) records conditions on 13 of 22 accepted sets (nine TRAIN, four validation), including Longest Road transfers and reverse-order bank trades. A future audit should fact-check those alternatives or mark the cases ambiguous.

A zero-update four-anchor preflight found exact target/menu joins, finite accepted-set losses, useful finite trainable gradients, zero frozen gradients, and unchanged parent weights. Two matched arms then each completed 60 updates from the same seed-11 checkpoint on the same TRAIN anchors: one used audited accepted sets and the other cached H singletons. Graph, GRU, action encoder and first turn-head layer remained frozen. The fit took about 14.5 seconds. No PPO, reward change, or EVAL label was used.

All three models chose the same current action on all seven anchors. On the four trade-route cases, the audited arm chose BUILD_ROAD three times and END_TURN once. It slightly increased accepted probability on the sole rejected END case without changing that action, while its mean accepted-set CE was worse than both controls. The frozen gate required CE improvement of at least 0.02 and two more accepted-set hits than each control, including a trade-route case. It failed. The unchanged parent already hit six of seven sets, so the plus-two hit requirement was **arithmetically unreachable before fitting**; this is also a protocol headroom flaw. All 16 label-validation episodes, including 594 forced own rows, were replayed; non-target agreement covers the other 1,075 nonforced rows. No smoke or fresh game screen ran and neither fitted checkpoint was promoted.

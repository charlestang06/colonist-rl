# Next large learning experiments

This program follows the failed independent opening confirmation, the failed raw-teacher H gate, the failed large raw-label BC fit, and the public-history critic's failure against a simple public control. Its purpose is to move decisions and full-game wins, not to maximize imitation accuracy. Experiments use separate board roles and share the simulator/adapter `Observation` contract. No result is claimed by this plan.

| Priority | Mechanism | First falsification | Advancement evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1, parallel | **Outcome-trained second settlement.** Learn a public candidate value from matched complete-game outcomes, with relative opponent production, scarcity, port conversion and expansion access. Keep all other actions raw. | [Prospective protocol](experiments/second-settlement-outcome-2026-11-06/PROTOCOL.md): 640 TRAIN counterfactual continuations across eight boards; exact public parity and terminal-win headroom before the 6,400-game fit. | Frozen model beats the fixed H-production and retired-surrogate candidate rankings on fresh counterfactual boards, then wins a fresh H pilot and a 96-board independent confirmation. |
| 2, parallel | **Public-consistent midgame decision search.** Reconstruct several hidden worlds from one public trade/pass/BUY/card view, pair root actions under common starting chance, and use win-first evidence to abstain on uncertainty. | Current 32-case first probe stopped mechanically before any completed counterfactual outcome because synthetic continuation resource beliefs violated a bound. Repair with a minimal public-ledger regression test, then use new TRAIN roles and an unchanged predeclared ranking question. | Material world diversity, exact root/observer parity, legal resolved continuations, changed roots in multiple families, blind public review and fresh complete games against raw/H/GNT. |
| 3, conditional | **Outcome-guided policy iteration.** Aggregate only high-confidence changed roots reached by the current student, train a factorized family/within-family actor with reviewed 68-column successor features, and compare to no-update and same-data base-actor controls. | First require either Track 1 or 2 to supply a source whose fresh game strength and decision coverage beat H. Check action encoding, teacher alternatives, gradients and trade-versus-END mass before fitting. | Fresh board-level wins against H and then authentic VF/AB, with the distilled actor checked against its own teacher. PPO waits until a qualified actor still has an identified long-horizon weakness. |

The opening experiment is the fastest independent strength route: second placement has a causal intervention point, a small public action menu and no learned-policy distribution shift after the root, because roads and midgame remain raw. The prior opening surrogate's +0.28125 VP but zero confirmation win difference is a reason to train directly against terminal wins and to compare to the simple production control. A modest 16-board pilot is a rejection screen; its positive result cannot substitute for the 96-board fresh confirmation. The older 32-board native panel was too uncertain to establish superiority, so native claims remain conditional and separately tested.

Track 2 owns midgame reconstruction and does not wait for Track 1's labels. It must not create perfect-information policy inputs: sampled opponent hands and deck are environment state only. Its first mechanical stop is useful because it occurred before ranking, so no result threshold was consumed. Track 3 is deliberately downstream of a qualified source. Repeating raw-label BC, the old recurrent critic, or the retired opening affordability formula would spend compute without a stronger target.

The shared experiment loop is: freeze source/seed roles and a small falsification, measure changed decisions and actual paired terminal outcomes, scale only the mechanism that survives, then confirm on independent boards. Record complete journals, failures, source/model hashes, legal action identity, runtime and per-board effects once per study. Keep final boards quarantined. An auxiliary VP margin may stabilize fitting, but terminal win remains the primary target and reward shaping, if ever used, gets a separate gameplay comparison.

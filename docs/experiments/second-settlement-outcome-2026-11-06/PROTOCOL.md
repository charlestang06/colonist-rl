## Public candidate and model contract

At a legal own second-settlement root, include the exact raw root first and at most four distinct public alternatives. Form a pool from the twelve highest fixed H-production legal sites, the best legal site for each of the five resources, and the raw root. Select the other four greedily to maximize minimum distance from the already selected sites in the fixed public vector of five local resource pips, five immediate second-settlement starting resources and five owned-port ratio improvements. Use L1 distance = sum of absolute five-pip differences + 3 times the sum of absolute starting-resource differences + 2 times the sum of absolute port-ratio differences. Stable H-production and then lower vertex id break ties. If fewer than five sites exist, use all. The pool rule, distance and tie order are code-frozen before any outcome. Record pool and legal-menu coverage; do not select candidates using labels.

For each candidate, the pure feature encoder uses its local and combined first-plus-second resource pips, public board resource scarcity, immediate starting resources, owned-port ratios, current opponent settlement production, remaining high-production legal sites, and access to unblocked expansion sites. It includes candidate-by-opponent-production, candidate-by-board-scarcity, candidate-by-port and candidate-by-seat interactions, plus the fixed H-production score and pick position, so a pairwise linear fit can learn context-dependent values instead of losing constant board fields when subtracting the raw candidate. It never receives a synthetic after-`Observation`; projected geometry is a feature, not a certified future menu. A fixed regularized pairwise linear win head learns candidate-minus-raw paired win contrast; a separate relative-final-VP-margin head is used only to break predicted win ties. Choose the raw root unless another candidate's predicted win gain exceeds the predeclared two-percentage-point threshold. The complete model file records column order, standardization, coefficients, fit data hashes, random seed and schema; loader rejects mismatches. A within-root shuffled-label fit is a leakage/optimization negative control, never an actor.

No checkpoint is promoted from agreement or regression loss alone. The fixed H-production root and the retired affordability surrogate's ranking on the **same candidate menus** are explicit counterfactual controls. The old surrogate is not retuned or used as a fallback in the candidate actor. Its diagnostic ranking uses a pure score adapter over these same five roots: call the evaluated `opening_draft.py` commit `de857d3c9a4804df37b7341d79df91e4b6b0582d` (file SHA-256 `585ddde614c532d42d27c545f518326ddcc2f0188ed91550a0f9446bee06c06b`) with its fixed seed 11, public H continuation and `pair_value` for each root; do not infer a ranking from its one selected action. The adapter and archived source bytes are hashed with the study manifest. On every non-second-settlement view, the actor returns the exact unchanged raw planner action object.

## Counterfactual data and privacy

For each selected root, save its full public `Observation`, ordered legal menu, exact public prefix and hashes before outcomes. The data runner reaches the second placement with the unchanged raw focal and persistent H opponents. Each of four feasibility or eight main-study independent world seeds starts from the same public setup state, resamples the unplayed development deck and future engine chance state without changing any public field or current ordered menu, and copies each opponent policy's state. All candidate roots within a world get the same starting sampled world, chance seed and opponent-policy states. Subsequent random streams may desynchronize when actions change their consumption; they are paired starting conditions, not asserted identical future dice traces. Execute the saved exact root `Action`, then the unchanged raw focal/H opponents to a resolved terminal game. All policy inputs after the root are seat-specific `Observation`s. The model trainer receives only public features plus sanitized terminal win and final relative VP margin; private deck/hands remain in the sampler. Any parity failure, illegal action, truncation, or deadline preserves the failure and stops that role without replacement.

The primary label and evaluation quantity is terminal win. Final VP minus the strongest opponent's final VP is a secondary tie signal, clipped to [-10, 10] for training. No dense reward is added to the engine. Paired contrast, candidate coverage, decision changes, legal completion, selection latency and board-level uncertainty are reported separately. There is no claim that a counterfactual rollout label is an optimal action.

## Frozen decisions

1. **Mechanical preflight:** exact root/menu identity, public feature invariance under hidden deck/chance perturbation, candidate-set determinism, original action execution, observer privacy, and same-world root-order invariance on synthetic fixtures. Failure stops before TRAIN feasibility.
2. **TRAIN feasibility:** all selected counterfactuals resolve without illegal action or parity loss; at least eight of the 32 second-settlement states on at least four boards have an alternative whose terminal win differs from raw in at least one matched world. Fit the fixed win head leave-one-board-out and report changed roots, aggregate paired win contrast versus raw and a within-root shuffled-label negative control. If it changes at least four roots and has nonnegative paired win contrast, continue normally. If this small learning check is weak but the specified outcome headroom exists, one predeclared escalation to the separate 40-board TRAIN fit is allowed with identical features, candidate rule and threshold; report the escalation explicitly. If headroom is absent or mechanics fail, stop. This is a feasibility signal check, not a strength claim.
3. **TRAIN fit / label validation:** fit only on TRAIN roles. Select the one fixed regularization from {1, 10, 100} by leave-one-board-out paired win contrast within TRAIN; freeze all model bytes before label validation. On the 16-board label-validation set, require all 2,560 continuations resolved, at least eight changed chosen roots on four boards, learned-minus-raw at least +8 net wins across 512 paired world comparisons, no negative mean relative-VP margin, and lower board-balanced paired regret than both the fixed H-production and retired-surrogate candidate rankings. Regret is the best sampled candidate win frequency on each state minus the frozen policy's chosen candidate win frequency; compare secondary VP regret only after primary win-regret ties. A failed term stops before H pilot; no same-panel model selection.
4. **H pilot:** all 192 games resolve. The learned arm must win at least four more of its 64 games than **each** raw and focal H and must not lower mean final VP by more than 0.1 against raw. Review every changed second root blind to arm/outcome for an obvious public error. Passing earns the previously sealed H confirmation; no pilot retune.
5. **H confirmation:** all 1,152 games resolve; learned win rate must exceed both raw and H by at least eight percentage points and both board-paired simultaneous 95% bootstrap lower endpoints must exceed zero (20,000 whole-board resamples, seed 20261106). Also require p95 second-placement policy selection ≤250 ms and maximum ≤2 s. If these fail, no promotion or native run. A pass nominates a working-player update and opens the conditional native comparison; it does not prove native superiority.
6. **Native comparison:** exact pinned VF and AB evaluation-only baselines with their documented shims; native choices are never labels. Predeclare a separate practical effect and simultaneous board-level rule before any native board. Do not claim native superiority based on the H panel.

Retain failures and negative panels. Do not pool PILOT/CONFIRMATION with TRAIN, recycle them as model labels, or adjust this source on exposed roles. The deployment adapter is outside this study; a positive simulator screen still requires an actual live-game extraction/execution check.

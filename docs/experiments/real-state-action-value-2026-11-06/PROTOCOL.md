## Question and independent experimental unit

Can a public actor trained on paired terminal consequences at real midgame Catanatron states improve trade, END, BUY, card and build choices, then beat raw and H in fresh complete games? The closed synthetic-world probe resolved 552 continuations but changed only 1/25 roots under all-candidate dominance. Independent analysis found root-dependent wins in 42/100 paired world slots and root-dependent VP in 98/100. This motivates expected action value and partial ranking across many real states, not a threshold change or use of those failed-panel labels.

TRAIN acquisition has two precisely defined occupancy arms on every board: (A) PublicTurnPlannerPolicy() with default depth 6, node cap 2048, beam 24, geometry cap 100000, seed 11 and gain margin 250000 micro as focal versus three persistent public HeuristicPolicy(board_seed + seat) opponents; (B) ConsequenceGraphPolicy(hidden_size=32, feature_set="hand_recipe") loaded from /Users/charlestang06/Documents/CS/colonist-rl/runs/swarm-2026-11-04/ppo-strength-preparation/seed-11-frozen-bc-3_3-start.pt, byte SHA-256 b3aae32ac36dc55f8ae26ec4676cc289243a6c540b959e1f8aa9786e5ddd0258, deterministic argmax and its own recurrent memory, versus the same H opponent type. All four focal seats are played for each arm, no domestic offers, standard rules and 20,000-step cap. A board, its eight source episodes, action branches and chance replicates remain one split/bootstrap unit. One own midgame decision per complete source episode can be selected. The three persistent H opponents are a fixed environment and later focal game control, never training labels or a private-state teacher. Their legacy belief sampler may use board-seeded randomness; call this H-legacy and report it separately from the focal actor's strict public-input guarantee. The raw and parent focal policy classes keep their original code/weights but receive the same interval-proxy focal view defined below. The observation-only H-interval sensitivity focal is HeuristicViewPolicy(seed=11) on that interval-proxy view; its tie RNG comes from the public view digest, and it is reported separately from H-legacy.

## Private replay, public selection and branches

Before a focal actor, candidate proposer, model or raw fallback sees a view, require the simulator-origin `Observation.bank` to be None and replace particle-sampled belief expected/variance with deterministic public interval proxies. The simulator currently emits bank=None; an exact engine bank inventory is never copied into the actor view, while bank-trade availability comes from the exact legal menu. For each opponent let w[r]=upper[r]-lower[r], residual=public resource_count-sum(lower), and expected[r]=lower[r]+residual*w[r]/sum(w) when sum(w)>0, otherwise lower[r]; use variance[r]=w[r]*(w[r]+2)/12 and exact=(lower==upper). Require each public count to be within the standard finite bank supply, sum(lower)<=public resource_count<=sum(upper), expected within bounds and abs(fsum(expected)-public resource_count)<=1e-12 after one deterministic last-flexible-coordinate residual correction; inconsistent inputs stop instead of clamping. Preserve lower/upper, own hand, action object identities, events and all other fields. This pure adapter is applied to every focal raw/parent/H-view proposal and learned action in collection, fit, label validation and gameplay; H-legacy opponents remain a fixed environment and do not define public-actor validity. The acquisition journal saves initial seed, all executed exact Actions, own public prefixes and ordered menus, with base terminal results in a separate private file. A deterministic selector reads each candidate's interval-proxy before-Observation, current legal menu and earlier own prefix, plus precommitted role ordinal, seat and source-arm stratum metadata. It does not read the current executed choice, later actions, base result or any hidden state; role/source metadata never enters actor features. For each exact legal root compute the reviewed public successor facts: a guaranteed direct win is `wins_now=1` from its point-gain lower bound, and multiple such roots use the lowest original-menu index. Exclude these views from acquisition and force that index at runtime. If no guaranteed win exists, abstain to raw when `point_outcome_uncertain=1` and current own VP plus that root's public maximum possible immediate gain can meet the target (BUILD_SETTLEMENT +3, BUILD_ROAD +2, BUY_DEVELOPMENT_CARD +1). ROAD_BUILDING is a later choice sequence, not an immediate current-root win. Every remaining eligible interval-proxy view receives a public focus family by SHA-256 of canonical before-Observation and ordered menu modulo its ordered eligible family list (trade, BUY, card, build). Each source episode receives a target family from (board role ordinal + seat + 2 * source arm ordinal) modulo four. Select the lowest public input hash among eligible views with that focus family, or the lowest among any eligible view if no target-family row exists. This rule is episode-local, so the first eight boards remain sealed when later TRAIN boards are added. It selects at most one case per episode, eight per board, and seals selected row/menu/prefix hashes before outcome branches. Family quotas below are stop gates, not post-outcome replacement targets. The episode-wide lowest-hash choice exists only in acquisition; at gameplay time the actor sees one current view and applies only the same public focus and candidate rule to it, without future views.

Replay a selected episode's exact action log from its initial seed to the pre-root state. On opponent turns, advance the same seeded HeuristicPolicy on its own Observation and require its action to match the logged action. On focal turns, recompute the declared raw or deterministic recurrent-parent action from its own interval-proxy public view and require that to match the logged action before applying it. Require exact focal Observation, ordered menu, hidden-state digest, simulator RNG and opponent RNG parity with the original snapshot. Deep-copy this real driver/environment for each branch. The candidate set, in order, contains the independently computed exact raw root, frozen-parent root, and the two roots of its public focus pair, with duplicate indices removed and no filler, so it has two to four original menu actions. For trade/END, choose the legal bank trade maximizing the tuple (largest recipe deficit reduction, negative deficit before, negative deficit after) across standard road, settlement, city and development-card resource recipes; require a positive deficit reduction, and break ties by lower original menu index. For BUY/END use those exact kinds. For card/comparator, choose a legal card root proposed by raw, then parent, then H-view in that priority; compare END when legal, otherwise ROLL. For build/comparator, choose the first legal city, then settlement, then road by original menu index; compare END when legal, otherwise BUY. If a core pair is unavailable, that focus family is ineligible before the public hash selects among families. A view with fewer than two distinct resulting roots is ineligible. The same exact rule runs in acquisition, label validation and runtime. Runtime publicly guaranteed wins bypass ranking; uncertain award wins abstain to raw.

A master future-chance schedule is committed before reading acquisition rows; derive four seeds per fixed board/seat/source/episode slot independently of Observation hashes, candidate actions and outcomes. Within each selected case, those four precommitted future-chance seeds branch every candidate from the same real hidden state, same opponent policy state and same starting chance state. Candidate roots use the replay view's exact ordered-menu Action instances at hash-bound indices, never synthesized equal-valued clones. Subsequent focal turns use frozen raw; each opponent receives only its own Observation. The future seed changes only the future RNG stream, never already realized hidden hands, deck order or public history. Different roots may consume random draws differently, so common starting RNG is not a claim of identical future dice. Four future seeds do not resample the already hidden opponent hands or deck; uncertainty over those comes from distinct real games and boards, and a single case is not an information-set oracle. The exact real hidden hand/deck is a label-generating environment sample only; the model input contains only the interval-proxy public Observation, exact Action, public candidate features and earlier own public prefix. Neither source arm nor board seed is an input.

## Targets and matched policy classes

Save terminal win and focal-minus-strongest-opponent final VP clipped to [-10, 10] for each resolved root/chance pair. Do not add dense engine reward. For each unordered root pair, average the four matched win differences. A nonzero mean trains its sign with softplus(-sign * (logit_i - logit_j)), weighted by absolute mean win difference. If mean win is tied and mean clipped VP difference is nonzero, train its sign with the same loss weighted by 0.1 * abs(mean clipped VP difference) / 20. Normalize pair weights to sum one per case; a case with all tied outcomes has zero loss and no update. Mixed-sign evidence remains a weak partial ranking; no globally dominant root is required. Report win and VP contrasts separately. A within-case shuffled-outcome fit is a negative control, never an actor.

The two matched trainable arms start from one exact parent checkpoint: full-actor ConsequenceGraphPolicy and reviewed zero-start CandidateSuccessorPolicy v3. Their parent action probabilities match at initialization. They use identical selected cases and current-weight full own-prefix replay: warm up all but the last 16 preceding own views under no_grad, preserve gradients through the last 16 preceding views and the current decision, then take four deterministic presentations per case, at most 768 updates. This matched 16-own-step truncated BPTT gives temporal gradients without a stale memory cache. Train all actor/graph/GRU/action/consequence parameters, but keep the value head frozen. Use Adam learning rate 0.0003, betas (0.9, 0.999), epsilon 1e-8, no weight decay and gradient norm cap 1.0, batch one selected state. Within each presentation, order boards by SHA-256 of the fixed epoch number and logical role ordinal, then seat and source arm; seed all framework RNGs from 20261106. No class-frequency weight, early stopping or labelVAL-based schedule change. Before any update, four audited anchors (one each trade/pass, BUY, card and build) must show exact menu/label binding, finite pairwise loss, a nonzero useful gradient in each trainable arm and zero gradients in frozen tensors. No stale recurrent cache. The pairwise loss and runtime choice mask every legal root outside the frozen candidate set before normalization; one selected bank root prevents the old many-bank-softmax multiplicity from silently changing family mass. The runtime overlay advances its current-weight recurrent memory on every focal decision, including raw fallbacks, so each eligible score sees the same complete own public prefix used in training. It scores only the frozen public candidate set and otherwise follows raw exactly. An equal learned score chooses the raw root when present, then the lower original menu index; the diagnostic ridge also retains raw on predicted-gain ties. The frozen parent restricted to that set, raw and focal H are controls. Actual bank, END, BUY and card argmax changes are separate readouts.

## Hard leakage and causality checks

Exact original-vs-replay focal Observation/menu and internal state/RNG parity; short original-action step parity for both occupancy arms and longer raw-source continuation parity; all branch actors see only their own Observation. Replay an identical complete visible-event trace and own hand through at least two independent BeliefLedger particle seeds, including a rejuvenation path, and require identical public lower/upper bounds before applying the interval proxy. Then perturb other seats' private hands, unseen deck or belief-particle RNG while holding the focal public facts fixed: interval-proxy view, candidate indices, public tensors and actor logits must be bitwise identical. Any seed-dependent bounds stop this protocol before a board; conditional invariance of the proxy alone is insufficient. A schema test rejects hidden hand/deck, board seed, source policy, future action, base result and private replay digest from actor data. The collector body-Observation SHA and the learner full-envelope SHA are separate bindings; conversion verifies both against exact saved bytes and never substitutes one for the other. Model/selector code must not be able to import the private replay file. All outcome and validation units split by whole board, not seat, branch or chance replicate. Candidate-set regret is explicitly not full-menu optimality. Failed mechanics or gates stop and preserve receipts; no PPO or same-panel retry. A positive simulator result still needs a separate Colonist screen-adapter check.

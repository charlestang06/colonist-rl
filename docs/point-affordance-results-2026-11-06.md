# Point-affordance feature: stopped before games

The [frozen design](point-affordance-feature-protocol-2026-11-06.md) added one
zero-start public point-gap scalar to the seed-11 consequence policy. Fresh
student play produced 24 TRAIN and 12 label-validation cases. Independent blind
review and exact-root engine checks supported the selected labels; the prefit
coverage and parent-headroom gates passed. A four-anchor gradient check passed
before two matched 48-update accepted-set fits.

On the fixed 12-case label-validation panel, accepted-set cross-entropy was
4.1314 for the unchanged parent, 4.0369 for the no-feature fit, and 4.0302 for
the feature fit. All three chose the same 12 roots and hit 4/12 accepted sets.
The feature made **zero actual accepted bank-trade rescues**; its loss gain over
the matched fit was 0.00677, below the frozen 0.02 gate. Across all 2,000
nonforced validation rows, feature and no-feature choices were identical.
**No smoke or development games ran, and no model was promoted.**

The learned scalar was +0.01151. A separate TRAIN-only diagnostic found a
median coefficient of 5.574 would be needed for an audited omitted bank trade
to overtake the matched no-feature scorer if only that scalar moved. This is a
retrospective margin diagnosis, not evidence that a large global coefficient
would play well. The next learning investigation should test broad public-state
action-value or ranking supervision from a qualified teacher, with explicit
trade/pass/BUY controls and fresh opponent-strength gates. PPO remains deferred.

# Public one-round resource-wise forecast v3: fast, but no fresh win gain

The [frozen v3 protocol](public-one-round-resourcewise-v3-protocol-2026-11-06.md) replaced only the exact four-roll bank-trajectory calculation. It passed exact parity, latency, intervention-yield, blind public review and excluded smoke gates. On the frozen eight-board fresh development screen, **forecast, static and raw each won 11 of 32 games**. The required forecast gain was at least three wins over each control, so this v3 mechanism stops without promotion or further unchanged-policy games.

## Verdict and next experiment

V3 is a successful exact throughput optimization but **fails the predeclared development strength gate**: zero extra wins versus both controls, below the required three of 32. The slight VP increase is descriptive. Keep the wrapper opt-in; do not advance to VF/AB confirmation or run more unchanged v3 games.

A distinct future hypothesis should compare **trade now** with **END while retaining the option to trade on the next own turn** after public roll-conditioned production. The current four-roll score gives a trade-now root credit for future point affordability, but its END root does not get a delayed bank-trade option; bank trades remain available whenever resources and bank stock permit. In a fixed-board/no-opponent model, waiting may preserve flexibility and often reproduce the exchange later, while discard, robber and bank shortages can make earlier exchange preferable. The 13 END-to-trade first divergences, no same-turn point builds and flat fresh wins are consistent with this missing option value, but do not establish it as the cause. Freeze a new exact public comparison and disjoint intervention/follow-through gates before any further game screen; do not retune v3 on these outcomes.

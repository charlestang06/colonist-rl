# Student-state DAgger audit: no fit

The recovered seed-11 FULL15 student corpus supplied 64 TRAIN and 15 separate
label-validation cases on eight and four boards. Selection was public-only and
excluded all 88 previously judged student anchors. Blind packets showed the
current public Observation, exact ordered legal menu, and earlier own history;
they withheld the current action, later outcomes, cached H choice, and source
pointers. The frozen [fit protocol](student-dagger-conditional-fit-2026-11-06.md)
required at least four TRAIN accepted sets that excluded the cached H singleton.

After corrected resource descriptions and independent strategic review, **20
TRAIN labels across eight boards and 15 episodes** and **five label-validation
labels across four boards and five episodes** remained supported. Count and
coverage passed, but only **3 of 20 TRAIN** accepted sets excluded H, across
two boards. No label-validation set excluded H. The disagreement gate failed,
so **no gradient preflight, fit, checkpoint, or gameplay comparison** used these
labels. Ambiguous cases were not changed after the H join.

The pinned-engine factual review found the selected point-building routes
feasible in synthetic public-consistent states. It checked core board fields
and exact ordered root legal menus, then selected follow-up build options. It
did not reproduce original hidden states, compare every Observation field or
entire post-action menu, or establish that a feasible route was strategically
best. Six raw judgments used incorrect resource names; separate rejudgments
corrected them while preserving the originals. A separate public Longest Road
fact check confirmed the four claimed road roots reached length five and the
two-point award. The final H joins were made only after public accepted sets
were sealed.

The next learning investigation should first establish a **new** source of
audited H disagreements. Public planner overrides on student-reached states
are a candidate, especially deterministic point-building routes. Select and
judge them under a separate frozen protocol, then stop before fitting if
coverage, route validity, or H-disagreement yield is insufficient. A route
certificate is not a training label by itself. Keep earlier FULL25 gameplay
regression and unchanged-parent retention as explicit controls. No evaluation
outcome may become a label.
